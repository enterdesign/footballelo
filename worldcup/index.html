ELO Formula

R = R + K × (S − E)

E = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_opp − R_self) / 400))

S: 1 = win  ·  0.5 = draw  ·  0 = loss

Base ELO = 1600   (all teams start equal)

K-Factors

  • Group stage → K = 8
  • Round of 16 → K = 16
  • Quarter-finals → K = 16
  • Semi-finals → K = 24
  • 3rd place match → K = 24
  • Final → K = 32

Notes

  • Penalty shootouts counted as draws (90+ET score used)
  • Successor states inherit predecessor ELO
  • All tournaments from 1930 included

Coverage

  • 1930 — 2022 (22 editions)
  • 85 national teams
  • Regions: UEFA · CONMEBOL · CONCACAF · CAF · AFC · OFC
  • Germany includes West Germany ELO history
  • Russia includes Soviet Union ELO history
  • † = defunct nation (no successor)
