The current look (below) uses the very common "dashboard" recipe: a condensed display face, letter-spaced monospace uppercase micro-labels, an orange accent and rounded glass cards. The three proposals drop the monospace labels and the orange, use one restrained accent, ordinary sentence-case labels, thin printed-style rules instead of cards, and tabular figures for the ratings.
